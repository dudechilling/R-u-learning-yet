# What is the difference between descriptive and inferential statistics?

## Immediate answer

Descriptive statistics summarize observations actually collected. Inferential statistics use data and assumptions to draw qualified conclusions about a larger population or process.

## Exam answer pattern

Identify the source observations, then ask whether the claim stays within the sample or extends beyond it. If it extends, name the required uncertainty and sampling assumptions.

## What to check

- An observed average alone does not estimate uncertainty.
- A nonrandom sample may limit generalization even with a narrow confidence interval.

## Interpret your results

A bar chart showing this class's responses is descriptive. An estimate of all students' responses is inferential and needs sampling evidence.

## Learn more

Inference is not simply 'more advanced arithmetic'; it depends on the design and model connecting the observations to the target.
