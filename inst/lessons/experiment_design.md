# How do observational studies differ from randomized experiments?

## Immediate answer

In an observational study, investigators measure existing exposures without assigning them. In a randomized experiment, treatment assignment is controlled by a random mechanism to reduce systematic confounding.

## Exam answer pattern

Identify whether investigators assigned exposure, whether assignment was randomized, and what comparison the design supports.

## What to check

- Random assignment does not guarantee a random population sample.
- An association in an observational study does not alone prove causation.

## Interpret your results

Randomization helps isolate treatment effects within the experiment, subject to compliance, measurement and other design issues.

## Learn more

Blinding, control groups, replication and measurement rules address different threats to interpretation.
