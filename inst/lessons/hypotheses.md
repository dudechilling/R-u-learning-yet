# How do I write null and alternative hypotheses?

## Immediate answer

H0 and H1 state competing claims about a defined population quantity or model. A statistical test assesses the incompatibility of observed evidence with H0 using a specified decision rule.

## Exam answer pattern

Name the population parameter, null value, alternative direction and significance rule before examining a result.

## What to check

- Not rejecting H0 does not prove it true.
- Do not choose test direction after seeing the results.

## Interpret your results

Your test result is conditional on assumptions and the defined null model; it is not automatically an effect-size estimate.

## Learn more

In a written response, distinguish a population claim from the sample statistic used to test it.
