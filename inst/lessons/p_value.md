# What does a p-value tell me?

## Immediate answer

Under a given null hypothesis model, the p-value is the probability of a test statistic at least as extreme as the observed one, according to the specified test. It is not the probability that H0 is true.

## Exam answer pattern

Define the null, statistic, alternative and assumptions, then explain the unusualness of the result under H0.

## What to check

- A small p-value does not measure practical effect size.
- A large p-value does not prove no effect exists.

## Interpret your results

The result describes model-based evidence against the stated null, not proof of causation.

## Learn more

Multiple testing, design defects and unmodeled dependence affect interpretation.
