# How do I recognize probability and nonprobability sampling?

## Immediate answer

Probability sampling uses a selection mechanism with known, nonzero inclusion probabilities for the target units. Convenience and volunteer samples do not supply those probabilities merely because the group is large.

## Exam answer pattern

Describe how each unit was selected, whether every target unit had a chance, and whether the method matched the research question.

## What to check

- Random assignment to treatments is different from random selection of participants.
- A convenience sample can describe those volunteers without representing everyone.

## Interpret your results

The selection process, not the final count alone, determines what population inference is justified.

## Learn more

Simple random, stratified, cluster and systematic sampling have distinct designs and dependence implications.
