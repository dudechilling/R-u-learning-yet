# What are Type I error, Type II error and power?

## Immediate answer

Type I error means rejecting a true H0, with long-run risk alpha under the testing assumptions. Type II error means failing to reject a false H0 for a particular alternative. Power is the chance of rejecting H0 when that alternative holds.

## Exam answer pattern

Identify the true/false hypothesis and the observed reject/not-reject decision before labeling the errors.

## What to check

- Power is not P(H1 is true).
- Alpha is a procedural error rate, not the chance a given rejection was false.

## Interpret your results

The costs and frequency of incorrect decisions depend on the studied problem and test design.

## Learn more

Power calculations require a specified alternative effect, sample size and variability.
