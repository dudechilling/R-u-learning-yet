# How do stratified and cluster sampling differ?

## Immediate answer

Stratified sampling separates a population into nonoverlapping groups and samples within each. Cluster sampling selects whole groups, often measuring many members inside selected clusters.

## Exam answer pattern

Identify what was selected at each stage and whether the groups represent strata or sample units.

## What to check

- Clustering can reduce independence among sampled observations.
- Strata should cover the target population without overlapping units.

## Interpret your results

Sampling from every defined stratum is different from sampling only some clusters. Weighting may be needed to represent the population.

## Learn more

Systematic sampling selects at an interval from an ordered frame and can be biased when that ordering aligns with a repeated pattern.
